## **2\. What are cookies and similar technologies?**

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website or use an online service. They allow a website or service to remember information about your visit, device, session or preferences.

Similar technologies include local storage, session storage and other browser-based storage mechanisms. These technologies are not always cookies, but they can store or access information on your device in a similar way.

In this Cookie Policy, we refer to cookies and similar technologies together as “cookies and similar technologies”.

## **3\. How we use cookies and similar technologies**

Aksum uses cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

* to operate the website and browser-based platform;  
* to remember user-requested language preferences;  
* to support user authentication, sessions and account security;  
* to prevent unauthorized access and protect the service;  
* to maintain access controls and platform functionality;  
* to provide features requested by users.

We do not currently use cookies or similar technologies for analytics, behavioral advertising, retargeting or marketing tracking.

## **4\. Strictly necessary technologies**

Strictly necessary technologies are required to operate the website or platform, provide requested features, authenticate users, maintain sessions, secure accounts, prevent unauthorized access and ensure the service works correctly.

These technologies may include:

* authentication and session technologies;  
* security technologies;  
* access control mechanisms;  
* browser storage required for login or session management;  
* technologies required to remember user-requested preferences, such as selected language, where used only for that purpose.

Because these technologies are necessary to provide the website or platform, they cannot be disabled through Aksum without affecting core functionality.

You may still block or delete cookies and browser storage through your browser settings, but doing so may prevent parts of the website or platform from working properly.

## **5\. Language preference technologies**

Aksum uses a language preference cookie called `sv-locale`.

This cookie stores the language selected by the user, such as English or Spanish. It helps the website and platform load in the selected language when the user returns and avoids unnecessary language switching on first load.

The same language preference may also be stored in local storage to synchronize the selected language across browser tabs.

These technologies are used only to remember the language selected by the user. They are not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or behavioral tracking.

## **6\. Authentication and session technologies**

When users access the Aksum browser-based platform, Aksum may use cookies, local storage, session storage or similar technologies to support login, user authentication, session management, account security and access control.

These technologies are strictly necessary for the platform to work. Blocking or deleting them may prevent users from logging in, staying logged in or accessing authorized areas of the platform.

Authentication and session technologies may be provided by Aksum and/or our authentication provider, currently Supabase.

Supabase may process authentication and session-related information in connection with providing database, authentication and storage services. More information about how Aksum uses service providers and handles international transfers is available in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

## **9\. Managing cookies and browser storage**

You can manage, block or delete cookies through your browser settings.

You can also clear local storage, session storage and other website data through your browser’s privacy or site settings.

Please note that blocking or deleting cookies and similar technologies may affect how the Aksum website or platform works. For example:

* deleting the `sv-locale` cookie may reset your language preference;  
* deleting local storage may affect language synchronization across tabs;  
* blocking authentication or session technologies may prevent you from logging in or using the platform.

Browser controls vary depending on the browser and device you use. You can usually find these settings under privacy, security or site settings.
